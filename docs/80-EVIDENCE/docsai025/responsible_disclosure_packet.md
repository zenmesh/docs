# Responsible Disclosure Packet — DOCSAI025

## Status: Draft — Awaiting Legal Review

## Security Reporting
Report security vulnerabilities to security@zen-mesh.io.

## What We Commit To
- Acknowledge receipt within 5 business days (target, not SLA).
- Investigate reported issues promptly.
- Communicate resolution timeline.
- Credit researchers who wish to be credited (subject to coordination).

## What We Ask
- Do not exploit the vulnerability or access customer data.
- Do not disclose the vulnerability publicly until resolved.
- Provide sufficient detail to reproduce the issue.

## No Bounty Promise
Zen Mesh does not currently offer a paid vulnerability bounty program.

## No SLA Promise
Response timelines are targets, not contractual guarantees.

## Legal Review Required
This policy page requires legal review before publication.
