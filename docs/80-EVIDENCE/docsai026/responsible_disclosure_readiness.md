# Responsible Disclosure Readiness

## Status: Draft — Legal Review Required
## Contact: security@zen-mesh.io
## Policy: https://zen-mesh.io/responsible-disclosure

### What We Commit To
- Acknowledge receipt within 5 business days
- Keep reporter informed of progress
- Credit researchers who wish to be identified
- Not pursue legal action against good-faith researchers

### What We Do NOT Promise
- Response time SLA
- Bug bounty payment
- Public disclosure timeline commitment

### Legal Review Needed
- Wording approval before publication
- Liability boundary confirmation
