# Privacy Policy

## Draft Content for Review

### 1. Information We Collect

#### Information You Provide

- Account registration details (name, email, company)
- Billing information (processed by Stripe — Zen Mesh does not store full payment card details)
- Webhook configuration and resource metadata
- Support communications

#### Information Collected Automatically

- Usage data (API calls, delivery metrics, feature usage)
- Log data (IP address, user agent, timestamps, error events)
- Analytics data via Vercel Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights (anonymized, aggregated)

#### Webhook Payload Data

- Webhook payload content is processed and stored as part of the service
- Payload data is encrypted at rest and tenant-scoped
- Raw payloads are not used for model training or analytics
- See [Data Handling](/docs/security/data-handling) for retention and access policies

### 2. How We Use Information

- To operate, maintain, and improve the Service
- To process transactions and manage billing
- To communicate about the Service (updates, security notices, support)
- To detect and prevent abuse, fraud, or unauthorized access
- To comply with legal obligations

### 3. Information Sharing

Zen Mesh does not sell personal information. We may share information:

- With service providers who help operate the Service (e.g., Stripe for billing, GCP for hosting)
- To comply with legal process or enforce our rights
- With customer consent
- In connection with a business transfer (merger, acquisition, sale)

### 4. Data Retention

Retention periods are described on the [Data Handling](/docs/security/data-handling) page.
Data is automatically and permanently purged after the retention period expires.

### 5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

- Access personal data we hold about you
- Request correction or deletion
- Restrict or object to processing
- Data portability
- Withdraw consent (where processing is based on consent)

To exercise these rights, contact [support@zen-mesh.io](mailto:support@zen-mesh.io).

### 6. Data Transfers

The SaaS control plane operates from GCP Toronto, Canada. Data-plane entry point
provider and region are to be confirmed. EU and APAC data planes are planned.

### 7. Security

Zen Mesh implements technical and organizational measures to protect data.
See [Security Overview](/docs/security/) for details.
