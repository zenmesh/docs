# Cookie & Tracking Policy

Zen Mesh uses a minimal set of cookies and tracking technologies. This page explains what we use and how you can control it.

## Essential Cookies

These cookies are required for the service to function:

- **Session cookies** — used for authentication and maintaining your logged-in state
- **CSRF protection** — used to prevent cross-site request forgery attacks

Essential cookies cannot be disabled without losing access to authenticated features.

## Analytics

Zen Mesh uses **Vercel Analytics** and **Vercel Speed Insights** to understand how the documentation and marketing site is used:

- **Vercel Analytics** — collects page views and navigation patterns
- **Vercel Speed Insights** — collects Web Vitals performance data (LCP, FID, CLS)

Analytics data is aggregated and anonymized. We do not use analytics to track individual users across sessions.

## What We Do Not Use

- **No third-party advertising cookies** — we do not serve ads or use ad-tracking networks
- **No social media tracking pixels** — we do not embed tracking from social platforms
- **No behavioral retargeting** — we do not track you across other websites

## Your Controls

You can control non-essential tracking through your browser settings:

- Disable JavaScript-based analytics via browser extensions or content blockers
- Clear cookies at any time through your browser settings
- Use browser privacy features (e.g., Firefox Enhanced Tracking Protection, Safari ITP)

Disabling analytics does not affect your ability to use Zen Mesh services.

## Changes

We may update this policy as our tracking practices evolve. Significant changes will be communicated through the documentation site.
