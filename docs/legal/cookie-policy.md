## Draft Content for Review

### Essential Cookies

These cookies are required for the Service to function:

- **Session cookies** — used for authentication and maintaining your logged-in state
- **CSRF protection** — used to prevent cross-site request forgery attacks
- **Load balancing** — used for session affinity (if applicable)

Essential cookies cannot be disabled without losing access to authenticated features.

### Analytics and Performance

Zen Mesh uses third-party analytics services to understand how the documentation
and marketing site is used:

- **Vercel Analytics** — collects anonymized page views and navigation patterns
- **Vercel Speed Insights** — collects Web Vitals performance data (LCP, FID, CLS)

Analytics data is aggregated and anonymized. We do not use analytics to track
individual users across sessions.

### What We Do Not Use

- **No third-party advertising cookies** — we do not serve ads or use ad-tracking networks
- **No social media tracking pixels** — we do not embed tracking from social platforms
- **No behavioral retargeting** — we do not track you across other websites
- **No cross-site tracking** — we do not share data with third parties for their own purposes

### Your Controls

You can control non-essential tracking through:

- Browser settings — clear cookies or block third-party cookies
- Browser privacy features (e.g., Firefox Enhanced Tracking Protection, Safari ITP)
- Browser extensions or content blockers for JavaScript-based analytics

Disabling analytics does not affect your ability to use Zen Mesh services.
