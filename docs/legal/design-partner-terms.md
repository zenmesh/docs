# Design Partner Program Terms (Draft)

## 1. Program Overview

The Zen Mesh Design Partner Program is an **inbound-only** program. There is no default pilot program. Participation is by invitation or expressed interest, subject to evaluation.

## 2. Eligibility

- No guaranteed acceptance
- Prospective partners express interest
- Zen Mesh evaluates fit and capacity

## 3. Program Benefits (Proposed)

- Up to 6 months of Pro plan access at no cost
- Direct feedback channel to the product team
- Early access to new features (where appropriate)

## 4. Obligations

Design partners are expected to:

1. **Complete a monthly online survey** starting from Day 1
2. A completed survey unlocks the next month of free Pro access
3. Maximum program duration: 6 months
4. Provide honest feedback on product experience

## 5. Survey Requirements

- Frequency: Monthly
- Format: Online survey
- Deadline: End of each month
- Consequence of non-completion: Program benefits suspended until survey is completed
- Maximum extension: No extensions beyond 6 months total

## 6. Limitations

- No SLA or support promise beyond standard Pro support
- No guarantee of continued free access after 6 months
- No guarantee of feature requests being implemented
- No exclusivity or priority treatment beyond survey feedback channel

## 7. Termination

Either party may terminate participation at any time. Upon termination:
- Free Pro access ends
- No refund or compensation
- No ongoing obligations except confidentiality of non-public information

## 8. Legal Review Required

These terms are a draft and **pending legal review**. No binding commitments are made.
