# Data Processing Agreement

> **Draft placeholder — pending legal review. Not effective until published as final.**
>
> A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) will be available for customers who require one.
> DPA processing is planned as manual at launch. Automated self-serve DPA generation
> is a future goal, not a current capability.

## Intended availability (pending launch)

- **Pro customers:** DPA available by request
- **Enterprise customers:** DPA included in the engagement
- **Free customers:** DPA is not available on the Free plan

## Requesting a DPA

Contact [support@zen-mesh.io](mailto:support@zen-mesh.io?subject=DPA%20request) with the subject "DPA request" and include:

- Your tenant ID
- Your company name and jurisdiction
- Any specific DPA requirements or templates

Do not rely on this page as a binding offer. DPA terms will be confirmed individually.
