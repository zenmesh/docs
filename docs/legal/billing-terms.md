## 2. Payment Processing

Payments are proposed to be processed through **Stripe**. Prices are USD-based with multi-currency support. Checkout and billing portal are **not yet live** — runtime implementation is pending.

## 3. Plan Details

- **Free:** Intended for evaluation and low-volume webhook delivery
- **Pro:** Increased retention, more destinations, priority support
- **Business:** Coming soon — expanded features and support
- **Enterprise:** Contact/evaluation only — custom terms and SLAs to be negotiated

## 4. Early Bird Pricing

Early bird pricing ($29/month, $290/year) is an introductory offer and may change. No guarantee of perpetual pricing is made.

## 5. Refund Policy (Proposed)

The specific refund and cancellation policy is **pending legal review**. The following principles are proposed:

- Monthly plans: prorated refund may be available upon cancellation
- Annual plans: prorated refund may be available in the first 30 days
- No refund for account termination due to AUP violations

These are draft principles and not binding commitments.

## 6. No Paid-Live Claim

The billing and checkout system is not yet live. No "billing live" or "paid Pro live" claim is made.

## 7. Changes

Plan pricing and terms may change with notice. Material changes are expected to be communicated before taking effect.

All billing, refund, and plan terms are **pending legal review**.
