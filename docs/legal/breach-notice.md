# Breach Notification Language (Draft)

## 1. Notification Commitment

In the event of a data breach affecting customer data, Zen Mesh intends to notify affected customers and relevant authorities **without undue delay**.

## 2. Timeline

"Without undue delay" means as quickly as reasonably possible after Zen Mesh becomes aware of a breach. This is **not** a specific time guarantee (e.g., "within 72 hours"). No SLA on breach notification timing is claimed.

## 3. Notification Scope

Notifications are intended to include:

- Nature of the breach
- Categories of affected data
- Approximate number of affected individuals (if known)
- Steps taken to contain and remediate the breach
- Recommended actions for affected customers

## 4. Contact

- Security incidents: security@zen-mesh.io
- Customer inquiries: support@zen-mesh.io

## 5. No Guarantee

No guarantee of specific detection time, notification time, or remediation time is made.

## 6. Legal Review Required

The specific breach notification procedures, timelines, and legal obligations are **pending legal review**. Applicable laws (PIPEDA, GDPR if applicable, state breach notification laws) will determine final requirements.
