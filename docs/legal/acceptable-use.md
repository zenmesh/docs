# Acceptable Use Policy

## Draft Content for Review

### Prohibited Uses

You may not use the Service for:

1. **Illegal activity** — any use that violates applicable law or regulation
2. **Abuse of webhook delivery** — sending spam, phishing content, malware, or other harmful payloads through the Service
3. **Infringement** — transmitting content that infringes intellectual property rights
4. **Service abuse** — attempting to bypass rate limits, resource limits, or security controls; conducting denial-of-service attacks; reverse engineering the Service (except as permitted by open-source licenses)
5. **Unauthorized access** — attempting to access other tenants' data, or using the Service to probe or scan third-party systems without authorization
6. **Resale** — reselling the Service without written authorization (except as permitted in the applicable plan)
7. **High-risk activities** — use in environments where failure could lead to death, personal injury, or property damage (the Service is not designed or intended for such use)

### Enforcement

- Violations may result in content removal, account suspension, or termination
- Zen Mesh reserves the right to investigate and take appropriate action
- Violations should be reported to [support@zen-mesh.io](mailto:support@zen-mesh.io)
- For security issues, see the [Responsible Disclosure](https://zen-mesh.io/security-disclosure) page

### Reporting Abuse

To report a violation of this policy:

1. Email [support@zen-mesh.io](mailto:support@zen-mesh.io) with subject "AUP Violation Report"
2. Include: date/time, description of violation, affected resources (if known), and any supporting evidence
3. Reports are reviewed and responded to within a reasonable timeframe

### Consequences

Depending on the severity and nature of the violation:

- **First violation (minor):** Warning and request to remediate
- **Repeated or severe violations:** Account suspension or termination
- **Illegal activity:** May be reported to appropriate authorities
