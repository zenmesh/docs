**No data loss on upgrade:** Your flows, targets, and evidence persist across tiers

## Common Questions

**Is Free secure?**

Yes. Free tier includes signature verification, delivery evidence, replay, and HTTPS enforcement. You get safe defaults for webhook security.

**What's the difference between Free and Pro+?**

Pro+ adds multiple targets, IP allowlist/block, per-target rate policies, and security alerts. These are the conversion features that enable multi-target setups.

**Is AI Transform available in Free?**

No. AI Transform is a Business+ feature (post-V1). Free tier focuses on deterministic webhook delivery.

**Can I upgrade anytime?**

Yes. Upgrade from Free to Pro+ to Business+ at any time. No data loss on upgrade.

**What happens to my data when I upgrade?**

Nothing. All your flows, targets, and evidence persist across tiers.

**Is customer AGE key support available in V1?**

No. Customer AGE key/BYOK is a Business+/V1.1 feature, not available in V1.
